What does “No limits on revisions” mean?

Once you choose your retainer plan, you can add as many design requests as you need to your queue. Simply prioritize them by importance, and we’ll work through them one by one. Once a request is approved, we move seamlessly to the next.

No limits on revisions. Just ongoing, on-demand support from your dedicated Dstudio design team.

How fast will I receive my designs?

Most requests are delivered within 2–4 business days, depending on the scope and complexity. Larger projects may take longer, but we’ll always keep you updated on progress and timelines.

Who are the designers?

Your work is handled by the DStudio internal design team. We assign the right specialists based on your project, supported by a Creative Director who oversees the work and ensures every deliverable meets our highest standards.

How does onboarding work?

Choose your plan and we’ll send your Slack invite within 15 minutes. Once you join, you’re ready to share your requests and start working with the Dstudio team.

Are there any refunds?

Due to the nature of our service and the level of design expertise we provide, all payments are non-refundable. However, our commitment doesn’t stop there—we’ll continue to support you and work closely with you to ensure you get the most value from your partnership with Dstudio.

What is the difference between Fixed & Retainer?

Fixed Projects are ideal when you have a clearly defined scope, timeline, and set of deliverables.

Retainers are designed for ongoing design support—whether you need us for 1, 3, or 6 months, or want a long-term design partner focused on helping your product or website achieve its goals, improve conversion, and drive growth.

Is there a trial available for Retainer?

Yes. You can experience our retainer service for one week for $1,850 USD—a great way to experience the Dstudio workflow, quality, and team before committing to a longer partnership.
